## How Does Rabby Wallet Work?

Rabby Wallet works as a browser extension and also offers mobile and desktop support for users who want broader access across devices. It connects to dApps, supports EVM-chain interactions, and helps users review transactions before signing.

One of its main differences from simpler wallets is that it emphasizes pre-transaction visibility. Instead of only showing raw signing prompts, Rabby is built to simulate transactions, highlight potential risks, and help users understand what may happen before approval.
