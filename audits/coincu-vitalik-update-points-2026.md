- `robots` index/follow setup
- self-referencing canonical for the current URL until a slug migration decision is made
- overall article structure with H1, H2, H3, and FAQ blocks
- relevant external references such as Arkham, Ethereum Whitepaper, Ethereum Foundation, Wikipedia, and X

## Update points that need action

### 1. Unify the article year and version

The page currently conflicts across multiple signals:

- slug uses `2024-update`
- HTML title still uses `2024`
- H1 outside the article uses `2025`
- the article now needs current `2026` framing if refreshed

This is the highest-priority update because it weakens clarity and freshness for both users and search engines.

### Recommended fix

- Immediate publishing fix: keep the current URL for now, but update all visible and metadata framing to `2026`
- Long-term fix: move to an evergreen slug such as `vitalik-buterin-net-worth` and add a 301 redirect from the old URL

### 2. Replace the title, H1, and meta description

Recommended replacements:

- Title: `Vitalik Buterin Net Worth 2026: ETH Holdings, Portfolio, and Wealth Breakdown`
- H1: `Vitalik Buterin Net Worth 2026: How Much Does Ethereum’s Co-Founder Own?`
- Meta description: `Updated for May 7, 2026: Vitalik Buterin’s known holdings include more than 224,000 ETH. Here’s a breakdown of his on-chain portfolio, recent ETH sales, and estimated net worth.`

Also synchronize:

- schema headline
- schema name fields
- breadcrumb title
- OG title
- Twitter title

### 3. Refresh the net worth section with current data

The article should no longer lean on 2024 or 2025 positioning for its main estimate.

Current-source notes for the update:

- Arkham's 2026 research says Vitalik Buterin holds more than `224K ETH`
- Arkham's 2026 research estimates his known net worth at at least about `$550M`
- Earlier in 2026, Arkham says his known ETH holdings were above `240K ETH`

### Recommended fix

Rewrite the main net worth section around a `known on-chain estimate` methodology rather than an absolute total wealth claim.

Suggested replacement copy:

> Vitalik Buterin’s net worth in 2026 is best understood as a public on-chain estimate rather than a fixed personal balance sheet. Arkham’s 2026 research puts his known net worth at at least about $550 million based on publicly attributed wallets and holdings.
>
> His known wealth remains closely tied to ETH. Because Ethereum represents the overwhelming majority of his tracked crypto holdings, his paper wealth can rise or fall sharply alongside the market.
>
> This estimate should not be treated as a complete picture of all assets he may own. Public wallet analysis may not include undisclosed wallets, private investments, or off-chain holdings.

### 4. Add the missing 2026 ETH sale context

This is one of the biggest freshness gaps in the current article.

Current-source notes:

- Arkham reported that Vitalik sold about `17,196 ETH` in early 2026
- Arkham valued that amount at roughly `$35M` at the time
- The sales were described in the context of ecosystem support, open-source software, privacy tools, and security-focused infrastructure

### Recommended fix

Add a dedicated section such as:

`Why Did Vitalik Buterin’s Holdings Decline in 2026?`

Suggested copy:

> One of the biggest updates in 2026 is that Vitalik Buterin reduced part of his ETH position through a series of public on-chain transfers and sales.
>
> Arkham reported that he sold about 17,196 ETH in early 2026, worth roughly $35 million at the time. The reported purpose of these sales was tied to ecosystem support, including open-source software, privacy tools, and security-focused infrastructure.
>
> This helps explain why his known ETH balance declined from more than 240,000 ETH earlier in 2026 to roughly 224,000 ETH later on.

### 5. Add a short methodology note

The current article needs a more defensible explanation of how the estimate is derived.

### Recommended fix

Suggested block:

> This estimate is based primarily on publicly known wallets attributed to Vitalik Buterin by Arkham. It should be treated as a known on-chain estimate, not a full accounting of all personal assets.

### 6. Fix only the clear editorial defects

The article does not need a full rewrite, but some visible defects should be corrected.

Minimum fixes:

- `Vitalik Buterin'S Ethereum Holdings` -> `Vitalik Buterin’s Ethereum Holdings`
- `Buterin'S ETH Holder Ranking` -> `Buterin’s ETH Holder Ranking`
- `Other Ethereum Co-Founder s` -> `Other Ethereum Co-Founders`

Also remove:

- repeated filler about ETH price volatility if the same idea has already been stated
- awkward or duplicated wording around time references such as `as of now`

### 7. Improve image alt text without changing the media

The images and charts can stay. Their alt text should be more specific.

Suggested alt text patterns:

- `Arkham chart showing Vitalik Buterin ETH holdings over time`
- `Vitalik Buterin on-chain portfolio breakdown`

The page still outputs `meta keywords`. This is not a major blocker, but it is low-value and can be removed if you control the template.
