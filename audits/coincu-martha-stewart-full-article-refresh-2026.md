## Who Is Martha Stewart?

Martha Stewart is an American businesswoman, author, television personality, and lifestyle entrepreneur. ## Early Life and Education

Martha Stewart was born Martha Kostyra on August 3, 1941, in Jersey City, New Jersey. She grew up in a Polish-American household where cooking, gardening, and homemaking were part of daily life, and many of the skills that later shaped her public identity began there.

She studied history and architectural history at Barnard College. Before becoming a full-time entrepreneur, Stewart also worked as a model and later as a stockbroker.
